Legal Agreement
Terms and Conditions for Publishing
1. Copyright and Ownership

You retain full copyright ownership of your work. By uploading, you grant Abay Repository a non-exclusive license to distribute and sell your work.

2. Quality Standards

All submitted works undergo quality review by our checker and maker teams. Books are scored on content quality (0-10), editorial quality (0-10), and technical quality (0-10). Minimum passing score is 7.0.

3. Royalties

Standard royalty rate is 70% of net sales after platform fees (10%). Payments are processed monthly on the 15th for the previous month's sales. Minimum payout is 100 ETB.

4. Prohibited Content

We do not accept plagiarized content, hate speech, illegal materials, or explicit content without proper context.

5. Review Process

Books are first reviewed by checkers, then by makers for final approval. You will be notified of the decision via email and dashboard notifications.

6. Revisions

If your book needs revision, you will receive feedback. You have up to 3 revision attempts before the book may be rejected.

7. Removal

You may request removal of your book at any time, subject to existing sales and refunds.

Royalty Agreement
1. Commission Structure

Authors receive 70% of net sales after platform fees. The platform fee is 10% of the gross sale price.

2. Calculation Example

If your book sells for 100 ETB:
- Platform Fee (10%): 10 ETB
- Net Sale: 90 ETB
- Your Royalty (70%): 63 ETB

3. Payment Schedule

Royalties are calculated monthly on the 1st of each month. Payments are processed on the 15th for the previous month's sales.

4. Minimum Payout

The minimum payout amount is 100 ETB. Royalties below this amount will accumulate until the threshold is met.

5. Payment Methods

Payments can be made via bank transfer, Telebirr, or Chapa based on your preference.
